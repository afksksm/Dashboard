# Sampled Design Tokens — Mittal Communication CRM

Sampled from reference images (`ref-01` to `ref-05`) in `design/references/`.

## 1. Primary Colors
- `--canvas`: `#EEF1EA` (Pale sage-grey canvas background)
- `--surface`: `#FFFFFF` (White cards & floating panels)
- `--surface-2`: `#F7F8F4` (Subtle off-white inner cards & inputs)
- `--surface-3`: `#EFF1EA` (Hover state & skeleton shimmer)
- `--line`: `#E4E7DF` (1px hairlines & soft dividers)
- `--ink`: `#121410` (Primary deep black ink for active pills & headings)
- `--ink-2`: `#3A3D36` (Secondary text)
- `--ink-3`: `#6E7268` (Muted labels & eyebrow text)
- `--ink-4`: `#9A9E93` (Placeholders & disabled elements)

## 2. Accent Colors
- `--lime`: `#D5F65A` (Electric lime for primary actions & chart gradients)
- `--lime-strong`: `#B9E52F` (Hover fill for primary buttons)
- `--lime-soft`: `#EEFAC4` (Tinted highlight cards & avatar badges)
- `--lime-wash`: `#F7FCE3` (Background wash & glow)
- `--olive`: `#8FA336` (Previous period comparison dashed strokes)
- `--indigo-crm`: `#2E3B8F` (KPI sparkline graphs)

## 3. Feedback & Status Slabs
- `--pos`: `#1F8A3B` / `--pos-bg`: `#E3F6D5` (Completed sales & positive deltas)
- `--neg`: `#C23B22` / `--neg-bg`: `#FCE6E1` (Cancelled sales & negative deltas)
- `--amber-crm`: `#D99A0B` / `--amber-bg`: `#FFF3CF` (Paper bill backfill & warnings)
- `--orange-crm`: `#F28A1F` (Card transactions)
- `--mint-crm`: `#5ED6B0` (Other payment tenders)

## 4. Radii Scale
- `--r-xl`: `28px` (Floating sidebar, top bar, hero trend card)
- `--r-lg`: `20px` (Standard dashboard & feature cards)
- `--r-md`: `14px` (Inner item cards, form inputs)
- `--r-sm`: `10px` (Tags, status badges)
- `--r-pill`: `9999px` (Primary action pills, nav buttons)
